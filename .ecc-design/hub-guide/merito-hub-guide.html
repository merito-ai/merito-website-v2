Before you start

Four things to keep handy

@

Your registered email

Use the same email you shared with Merito at the seminar. Plans are unlocked for that email only.

✉

Access to that inbox

There is no password. We email you a sign-in link each time.

⌁

A laptop

Use a laptop with a camera and microphone, needed for the mock interview.

▤

Your CV and a job description

Your CV as PDF or Word (under 5 MB), and the full job description of a role you want.

01

Open Merito HUB

  • Go to www.merito.ai and click Hub in the top menu.
  • Or type merito.ai/hub directly.
  • Click the red Log in button (top right of the form card).

The form on this page is for a new free score. You only need Log in.

02

Enter your email

  • Type your registered email.
  • Click Send link.
  • No password, no OTP to type.
03

Check your inbox

  • The email comes from Merito Hub within a minute.
  • Not there? Look in Spam or Promotions.
  • Still nothing? Click Send a new link.
04

Click the button in the email

  • First time: "Confirm email and go to my dashboard".
  • Next times: the sign-in link email.
  • Each link works once and for a short time. Use the newest one.
05

You land on your dashboard

  • Look for Exclusive plans unlocked for you.
  • Unlock plans: see both plans and pay.
  • Check my fit for a role: add a role, job description and CV to get your score.
  • The right panel lists what your plan includes, step by step.

The role check is free and takes about a minute. Do it before or after you pick a plan.

07

Confirm, then pay

  • Click Choose this plan under your plan.
  • Check the summary, then click Continue to payment.
  • Pay securely with Razorpay (UPI, card, net banking).
  • The page refreshes and shows Your plan. Your receipt is in Order history.
08

Check your first role

  • Click Check my fit for a role.
  • Add your name, phone and the role you want.
  • Pick your seniority level carefully. It stays the same for every role you add later.
  • Paste the full job description, upload your CV, then click Check my fit for a role.

Your score out of 10 appears in about a minute. Add more roles from the role menu at the top right.

Step 09 · Use the left menu

Work through your steps

▤

Fitment report

Click Unlock full report, then Use 1 of your 3 plan reports, free. A 4th role report is ₹299.

◎

Personality test

104 statements in 2 parts, about 17 minutes. Progress saves after Part 1. Done once, counts for every role.

◉

45 min, medium difficulty, up to 10 skills from your role, with a scored report. One per plan. See the next slide.

☰

Reference checks

Click Start my reference check, add each referee's name, email and company (up to 10). 3 completed references give a verified report.

⧉

Consolidated report

Fills in as you finish each step. Download it as a PDF or share it by link.

✦

Mock interview

Before you start the interview

Pick the role first

The interview is for the role shown at the top right. Your plan includes one interview, and you can't switch its role later.

Start it

Open Mock interview, click Start my mock interview, then Start interview for your role. When ready, click Start Interview.

Set up

Laptop with camera and microphone, a quiet room and a stable network. Plan for 45 minutes in one go.

10 days, resume once

Finish by the date shown on the page (10 days). If it drops, you can resume once; a second drop can't be recovered. Your scored report appears on the same page.

Assess + Mentor + Apply

Mentoring and application help

Mentoring

Two 30-min sessions with a Merito expert. In Expert guidance, click Schedule your call for session 1 and pick a time in the Calendly window. Session 2 opens once session 1 is booked. After each call, rate the session right there.

Application help

Our team gets in touch to pick 3 companies with you, then refers you through Merito's network.

Track progress

Your dashboard shows each step as it moves to In progress and Done.

What we promise

We refer you to the companies you choose. We can't guarantee interviews or offers.

Questions · Signing in

Signing in

No email arrived

Check Spam and Promotions, wait a minute, then click Send a new link.

"Link expired"

Links work once and for a short time. Request a new one and use the latest email.

I don't see my plans

Is there a password?

No. Every sign-in uses a fresh link sent to your email.

Questions · Using Hub

Using Hub

Do I have to finish everything now?

No. Features never expire. Do the steps in any order, at your pace.

Can I add another role?

Yes. Open the role menu at the top right and click + Check my fit for a role. 3 role reports are in your plan.

Payment went through, plan missing

Refresh the page and check Order history. Still missing after 10 minutes? Write to us.

Interview got interrupted

Open Mock interview and click Resume. You can resume once.

Prepare now, Mentor later?

Yes. Upgrade from Assess + Prepare to Assess + Mentor + Apply anytime for ₹2,999.
